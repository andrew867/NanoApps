# A short focus block with a pink noise bed.
# carrier and noise apply to the seg lines that follow them, so a program can
# change either partway through.

name    Focus Block
mode    binaural
carrier 250
noise   pink 0.15

seg     14   17   1500     # 25 min ramp up through beta
seg     17   14   300      # 5 min back down to land softly
